# Gumroad Workflows follow-up sequence — for the AI Prompt Quick-Start lead magnet

**Status: drafted, not configured anywhere. No Gumroad account action taken.**

## Why this was built

`marketing/lead-magnet-freelancer-quickstart/EMAIL-SEQUENCE.md` (built
2026-08-08) established the pattern: a free lead magnet without a follow-up
sequence only collects an email address and goes quiet, capturing none of the
~42% of Gumroad sales that come from email/external traffic (see
`products/MARKET-NOTES.md`, 2026-07-20). That pattern was only ever built for
the freelancer line. This lead magnet (`Small-Business-AI-Prompt-Quick-Start.pdf`,
built 2026-08-11) needs the same thing so it isn't a repeat of the same
"collects an address and stops" gap on the other product line.

## Sequence design

Same 3-email, real-elapsed-time structure as the freelancer sequence, adapted
to the AI Prompt Playbook line's own products:

1. **Immediate (0 days)** — deliver + earn trust. One soft mention at the end,
   no hard pitch.
2. **Day 3** — a genuinely new prompt-use tip not in the free PDF, bridging
   into the full Small Business AI Prompt Playbook (Volume 1).
3. **Day 7** — introduces Volume 2 (a different problem — systems/automation,
   not one-off tasks) and spells out the bundle math plainly ($29 for both
   vs. $38 separately).

**Deliberately no discount code in this sequence** — same reasoning as the
freelancer sequence: LAUNCH25 (see `products/MARKET-NOTES.md`, 2026-08-04) is
scoped to Jimmy's personal-network message only. Firing a capped code at
anonymous downloaders risks exhausting it before it reaches people he
actually knows.
